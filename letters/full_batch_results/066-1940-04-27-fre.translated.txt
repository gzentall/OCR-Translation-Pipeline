My darling, Paris, April 27, 1940. I finally received your first letter this morning, dated the 22nd. It only left Pézenas on the 25th! Finally! The main thing is that you arrived safely and that you can start working again. I&#39;ve already written you four letters, but all to Postal Sector 2197; I don&#39;t know if they arrived. The most important thing is that Ege has arrived at the Carlton Savoy Hotel. Family and later, Gyula are here; he plans to come and see us. I went to see a great doctor who took an X-ray and is going back again this afternoon. He already told me that all my problems stem from my liver (Leber) and intestines (Gedärme) and that those must be treated first. I&#39;ll tell you exactly tomorrow. Ilford is in serious trouble. They&#39;re out of prepared developers, out of Tonnelle papers, they only have Bromide paper left, very expensive (110 for 100 18-24). Do you want that or do you want to try another paper? We make them ourselves. I went to see Michel. Sarah arrives in Paris tomorrow and she wants to call me next week. It&#39;s not going to happen on its own. You should have sent the money yourself. Now I&#39;m going to... I kiss you, my Robert Zweigenthal. (Regimen Telecostos Vel Rota Pezenas/ E 1995 29-4 40 ERAB)

Paris, December 25, 1940. My darling, I&#39;m not bad, I didn&#39;t write to you yesterday because, on the one hand, I was sick, and on the other hand, I had a lot of work—I was sick, well, I had a little attack, but very little. I didn&#39;t even stay in bed, and today I&#39;m much, much better! Yesterday I went to see a great specialist, who took an X-ray and told me some important things; that it&#39;s not the lungs that produce the microbes, but the liver (Leber) and the intestines (Decarme), the bacilli, and that the source needs to be treated. But that it&#39;s not serious at all, and in a month it will all be over! I have to go back to him next Saturday with 1 kilogram of Bismuth in my stomach (Bron), and he&#39;s going to repeat the examination before giving me the exact diet (Diet) to follow. Finally, he&#39;s a serious man (his prices reflect that: 300 francs for the radio, 60 for each visit). I trust him. I hope he&#39;ll cure me. Work is still going badly; I&#39;m always busy buying lab equipment and waiting for Winkler, who&#39;s never available. I haven&#39;t heard from you. Maybe tonight. I didn&#39;t have that letter receipt from dear Bobchen. If I had work, I would go. I don&#39;t have time. Since I don&#39;t want to think when I&#39;m here, I&#39;m not left alone for a minute, and when I&#39;m home, I&#39;m too tired. I&#39;m a little afraid of Sundays; I have to find something for those days. I called Spira. He wasn&#39;t there. I have to call again this afternoon. My darling, I promise to write to you every day now, same thing. I adore you and kiss you and I miss you terribly, your Baba- PARIS RP 15 30 26-IV 1940 D ART AVIATION IS THE MOST BEAUTIFUL OF CAREERS Robert Zweigenthal 10 1st Reg.

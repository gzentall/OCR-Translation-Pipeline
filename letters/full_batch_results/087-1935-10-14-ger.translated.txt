I am about to leave here and wanted to quickly write you a few lines. You know, even though you weren&#39;t there, my dearest, I have grown so fond of Bratislava that even in retrospect, all the wonderful memories are still very beautiful. Today I am traveling on to Carpathia, Russia. Since I don&#39;t know how to write to you, please come home. Everything will be possible for me very soon. And to my family, I congratulate you on the one from Pestry Vicen. How much did you get for that? – If you continue like this, you&#39;ll end up under my thumb, you very fine person. Probably not yet. But I can tell you that I also work quite well, even though I have such a fat wallet as you. But one of us has to have more, and right now it&#39;s you. Just imagine how much I&#39;m happy for you. I was often with Ravel Weissmann. He sends his best regards. For the comfort of both of you, you&#39;ll hear from your Bob again soon.

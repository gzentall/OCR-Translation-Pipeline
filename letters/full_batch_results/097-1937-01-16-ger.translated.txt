I don&#39;t think you received the last one. (8 days after your phone conversation). At least that&#39;s what your letter suggests. Of course, I got the beautiful tie, and I kiss you a thousand times for it. And the reasons [illegible]: It wasn&#39;t simple. Since he hadn&#39;t been in the business, the whole time, and the wonderful work of the inventory and balance sheet remained. I started this work, and no one could have continued it. Postponing this work wouldn&#39;t have been possible for tax reasons. Egge&#39;s presence certainly wasn&#39;t decisive for my question. He wouldn&#39;t have finished the work, as he knew little from the last [illegible]. Darling, you scolded me so sweetly. Perhaps you were right about that. I don&#39;t know, as I can&#39;t judge. Believe me, I wish things were different. But my actions are forced upon me by a situation that, rationally speaking, allows for nothing else. It&#39;s perhaps the result of an overly selfish upbringing. A source of pleasure for the parents and a curse for the youth. A cautionary tale for us all. I also wrote to you about London in my last letter. Yes, I&#39;d have to travel by plane because I can&#39;t drive through Germany. My visa refusal has already been denied twice. I&#39;ll inquire about the options again in Prague. Flying with the French airline is too expensive. But I can only fly via Germany. I haven&#39;t been skiing this time. I probably won&#39;t get much of a chance. But that&#39;s so insignificant in the great chaos I&#39;m currently experiencing. The only bright spot at the moment is a nice, young, very talented director I met and became friends with. He&#39;s very capable and runs a modern children&#39;s theater in Prague. You know, with shows like &quot;Emil and the Detectives.&quot; He also does his own work. He feels exactly the same way I do. He&#39;s Czech but lived in Frankfurt. He&#39;s just lucky enough to be very rich and can work financially independently. He&#39;s also interested in filmmaking and has already written a lot. If you ever have anything, send it to me. I&#39;ll also give him the book &quot;The Cadavre en Fin&quot; to read. By the way, I told him a lot about you. Baba, the article is quite good, and I&#39;m glad you had success with it; keep sending me things like that. You&#39;ll get it back with your next letter. Maybe again soon.

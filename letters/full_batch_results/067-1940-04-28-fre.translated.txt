My Little Sunday, April 28th, 40th. This morning I received two letters, one from the 23rd and the other from the 24th. I&#39;m so glad everything is going well with you. I&#39;ll send you the photos you were sending tomorrow; they&#39;re so beautiful. But don&#39;t ask me for candles. I also make Ilford Bromide paper. You take care of the developer: Your 10 ri - The doctor gave me new medicine and I can eat a little chicken and ham. Today is for resting, I&#39;m regaining my strength, full of confidence - &amp; I&#39;m writing you 2 short letters to Heddy and Irma. Write every day, my darling Baby - to you, Baba. CORRESPONDENCE 2 PM TO THE ARMY. IT POSTAL FRANCHISE. SENDER: Mrs. E. Zweigenthal, 6 Rue Brancion, Paris 15th. Mr. Robert Zweigenthal, Fer R. 1. Czechoslovakian Vel Rota Pézenas Hérault

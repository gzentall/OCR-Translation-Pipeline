Paris, 1st III.40 My dearest, Finally, well after noon, the first letter in 11 days. It&#39;s my letter dated the 23rd and sent by censorship of 37. By morning I received my card dated the 26th and a letter from the 25th. Your letters of the 19th, 20th, 21st, 22nd, as well as the one from the 24th, are lost. It&#39;s truly terrible; I wonder if there&#39;s anything that can be done about it?! I&#39;ve already asked. My safe-conduct at the police station for next week. I wanted to go to... Now I&#39;m naturally postponing this trip, especially since you&#39;re coming to Paris around the 10th... Listen, Bobchen, I would very much like you to bring with you, if possible, the negatives of the first two reports. I would need quite a few prints. Mrs. Darle could do it, and you could take the films home with you. We&#39;ll print whatever you want from Avenue La Bourdonnais. You know, Mrs. Darle works very quickly, and you&#39;ll have everything you need in a few days. My little one, Mama is asking you for oil (your 2 liters) if you have any there. I&#39;m not sending you any films, I&#39;m sending them home. But do you want some money? At home, everything is fine, only you&#39;re starting to miss us terribly. I&#39;m already counting the hours, and I hope that by then I&#39;ll have a few more letters. I love you, my love, and I&#39;m waiting for you.

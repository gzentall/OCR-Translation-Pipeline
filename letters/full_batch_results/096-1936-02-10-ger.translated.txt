Prague, January 10, 36. Babūs, your letters took quite a while to reach me, but that made me all the more delighted. I spent a long time in the hotel, lying sick in my room, thinking of nothing. I was overjoyed to see you, more than anything. I never felt pained at having you with me in that moment. I certainly wouldn&#39;t have said much to you—just looked at you and felt happy. I will long for Zeinis for a long time to come. I look at the pictures almost every day and show them off. By the way, I&#39;ve taken some other photos myself; you&#39;ll get them in the next letter. I&#39;ve already been skiing again. I realized that I&#39;ve really learned a lot. If possible, I&#39;ll go out again on Sunday because it&#39;s snowed beautifully here the last few days. By the way, in 14 days, Egge is going to Germany. I hope he has good weather too and that he can still get Sparmarks in Strasbourg. He&#39;s feeling quite good here at the moment, as he has a nice girlfriend who works at the film studio. He&#39;s really a different guy since then. Tomorrow I&#39;ll inquire again at Elekta Film about work. Things haven&#39;t progressed for me yet, but there&#39;s no rush. In any case, I&#39;m also convinced that I shouldn&#39;t work for free if possible. But that also depends on the opportunity. I know that these are very rare, and that it&#39;s impossible without connections. But I just have to wait for that moment. I&#39;m so looking forward to it.

Prague, August 4, 1936. My dearest little Zabalein! – How happy I am, I can&#39;t write to you nervously – I can hardly count the days and am already quite nervous about Zabies myself. Understandably, I am in Prague when you come. You can&#39;t take this away from me, even if I still have urgent work to do. We must tell me exactly the train time for the day of your arrival. It is possible that I will be on the journey until the last day, so that I can be with you as much as possible. Get well soon, Babies, and eat well. Let your Bob take care of you.

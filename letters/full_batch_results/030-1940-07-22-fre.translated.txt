My darling, my dear, from Liginiac, July 22, 1940. Davos, I received your letter of the 17th. My biggest worry is also gasoline. It seems the government is looking into this, but it could take some time. Anyway, I&#39;ll do my best to get some. I also hope to get all the necessary permits, both for us and for the car. There&#39;s no reason why we shouldn&#39;t get them. This morning we received a wonderful letter from Lucien, which only took 10 days to arrive. It&#39;s extraordinary. He&#39;s working hard and very hard; things are going very well for him. They want to go on vacation soon. Johnny is doing very well. They&#39;re very worried about us, but in the meantime, they will have received all my letters (I wrote to them every 10 days). Lucien asks me if we have enough money, as he might even send us some. For now, we don&#39;t need any. I&#39;m very glad the letters are arriving. Lucien also told me I have to go to America. We&#39;ll talk about all of this when we&#39;re together. In any case, I&#39;ll never go alone. I&#39;m staying or going with you, and ONLY WITH YOU. Do you understand? I also received a letter from Mrs. Metzl this morning. She received a letter from her son in your handwriting, and she doesn&#39;t understand what it means. She advises us to stay here for the time being, while we wait for the fate of some people to be decided. Jules will also stay where he is now. I replied to her and asked her to inquire in Paris if it&#39;s possible to send packages to our home. If so, I&#39;ll ask Boris, our housekeeper, to send us your clothes. Boris also wrote to us; her husband is with her, and for the moment, everything is fine for them. She knows that several of your comrades are prisoners, not far from Paris, and they are very hungry... Pubi and his wife went to Hungary and are currently spending their vacation at Lake Balaton with the Lenard family. They managed.

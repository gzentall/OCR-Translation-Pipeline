Lignières, May 21st - My darling, I saw your telegram this morning in which you ask me for a medical certificate. I&#39;m sending you a copy of the certificate from Dr. Varadé. Explain to the police station that it&#39;s dated May 22nd, therefore on July 22nd I&#39;ll be in my 7th month of pregnancy, not my 5th. I hope that will be all right. If I have to get a new certificate, which I have to come and which will delay us by at least 48 hours, I&#39;ll still get it done and send it to you, but if this is enough, please wait. I&#39;m also sending you a proof of residence certificate that corresponds to the one in Paris. It might be useful. Everything is fine here. The mobilized men are starting to return, and there&#39;s more in the village every day. But none will be as happy as yours the day you hold her in your arms—Deep down, I received my little plush toy from you. I&#39;m now so ugly and fat, it&#39;s terrible. You won&#39;t love me at all anymore. And then there&#39;s the great misfortune of Blacky; here, there&#39;s no way to be careful, the country dogs are so &quot;fierce&quot; and badly behaved, and Blacky was so provocative, and Kitty is the same—Just at that moment we had so many other worries, it happened near the sad meadow?! My darling, I long so much to see you, to talk to you, it&#39;s terribly hard. But now you must be patient a little longer. Perhaps by the end of next week you&#39;ll already be at our place. Above all, don&#39;t do anything foolish at the last minute. My Bob, I&#39;m waiting for you and adore you. Your little wife, Mr. Robert Zweigenthal, 4th Good 17th Company, Camp d&#39;Agde, Agde. Mrs. Zweigenthal, Lignières (marital).

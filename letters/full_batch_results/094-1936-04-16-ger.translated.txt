On the train to &amp; earth 16. 4. 36 My dear little Zabalein. I&#39;m standing on the train right now and it&#39;s shaking terribly. If this continues, I&#39;ll have to give up my good intention of writing to you live. I couldn&#39;t continue writing, so the letter will only be finished in Chomutov. Zabalein, the weather is so wonderfully beautiful; everyone is smiling at you, people are in a better mood, and Bob would love to get up to some mischief with exuberance. Business is going well too. It seems that we are slowly but surely making a name for ourselves. But with this beautiful weather, a longing always awakens in me again, which is then broken. – I miss you so much, and simple, friendly attempts to compensate for all the young people&#39;s disappointment are unsuccessful. Write to me, Zabal, when you&#39;re going on holiday this year. We really must see each other again soon! – Or will you like it so much in England in the end that you won&#39;t want to come back?!! You must also tell me in detail about your work there and your circle of acquaintances. Your toe I am - Joh I am almost a little Rifersnelitio. Egge came back from Aflum yesterday without being in Paris. Apart from the fact that I thought it was stupid of him, he couldn&#39;t achieve anything there. He had to come back and will probably - unless we have extraordinary luck - be drafted into the military on April 21st. We have already come to terms with it and are trying to adjust completely today. Of course, I am even more indispensable at home now. The girl has almost completely recovered in the meantime. She is 29 years old and won&#39;t laugh, she&#39;ll be getting married in 2-3 months. But you don&#39;t need to be afraid, not me, but a rich, young, and handsome director general. She still loves me very much, but after I told her that I couldn&#39;t advise her to marry me, that she was after all 29 years old and had to think about marriage, she agreed with me with two tearful eyes. I&#39;ve already spoken with him and got the best impression of him – not your everyday life, but at least a happy ending. I think it&#39;s very nice to meet Trugard Hahn. I know her very well – yes, she was perhaps the Liesel Hite Jansen school to me. She&#39;s an extremely good friend, and I also think very highly of her abilities. She&#39;s destined for fashion, the most talented young girl I know. I hope she soon finds the right field of work so she can progress. I haven&#39;t received a letter from her. Do you know her address?! Little one, I long for her so much. Write to me soon, Zob.

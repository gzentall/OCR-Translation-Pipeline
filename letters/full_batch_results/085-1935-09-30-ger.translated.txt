8 days in Schönberg, September 30, 1935. I was at home this time, but only for 3 days involuntarily, as I had caught a bit of a cold. Now I feel better than before and am trying to work diligently. However, I will be staying longer on this trip, about 6 weeks. I am so far from Prague that it would be too tiring to drive back every Saturday. Your letter pleased me very much, especially your audacity, which you allowed yourself to do despite the censorship. Of course, I immediately went to the coffeehouse and admired your work; that is to say, I couldn&#39;t read it, but the photographs were pretty much the best that this paper had to offer. Very nice in the other newspaper. – I didn&#39;t find anything about E. You can imagine that I will always be happy when you are successful. But when it appears so clearly before you, it is something very special. Laci must have been very happy, very happy. I&#39;ll definitely keep my fingers crossed that your wish to earn at least 1000 francs a month comes true. But if it&#39;s not that much, don&#39;t be sad, darling. Rob will still love you. It&#39;s wonderful to have a lot of money, and it certainly contributes a great deal to complete happiness. Perhaps it&#39;s even necessary, but I&#39;ll never adjust to that. It makes one so easily spineless; I&#39;d even go so far as to say that character suffers as one&#39;s ego grows. Not that I condemn it, quite the contrary. It&#39;s simply a sobering fact. But we&#39;ve already discussed this at length. Right now, I&#39;m sitting in a nice little restaurant, waiting for my train, which will take me even further. I&#39;m traveling to Brno, Ostrava, and Bratislava in the next few days. But it&#39;s not just Bratislava that makes me happy on this journey, but that each day, each stop, brings me closer to the time when we can kiss each other tightly in our arms again. Yes, my love, you absolutely must make it possible for us to meet at Christmas. I don&#39;t know if I&#39;ll be able to find the time to travel later (from January 10th, 1936). That would be a real shame. And besides, with the time I have available, it&#39;s hardly possible to write (please excuse me, my train is coming soon). With love I therefore want to stop kissing. Please send any further mail by Saturday to the main post office in Moravská Ostrava, Czech Republic, for delivery to Prague.

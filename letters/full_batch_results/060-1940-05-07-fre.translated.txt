Paris, May 7, 1940 My darling, I am very unhappy with you. Do you no longer have time to write to me, do you no longer love me, or are two out of three of your letters lost? On Sunday I received two letters, but on Friday and Saturday I received nothing, and neither on Monday nor Tuesday. So that makes two letters in five days. It&#39;s not much. I am well. Work is going much too slowly for my liking, the laboratory is worthless, and those who work there are even worse. It&#39;s terrible! I will go see Vloek on the first day when I can escape the office for a bit, but you know very well that it is not very easy. There is always so much to do. I so wish you could take care of collecting this money. Well, I will try, but I foresee quite a few difficulties. Yesterday I sent you a small package; I hope you receive it. Tell me also if you absolutely need the products to make your developers, that is, if you can&#39;t get them there? In that case, I&#39;ll buy them from Ilford and send them to you. Only in that case will you absolutely need a scale to weigh the products very precisely. Now I&#39;m going to eat, I&#39;m leaving you, it&#39;s 1:20 and I&#39;m very hungry.

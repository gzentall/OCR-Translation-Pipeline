My Love, the 15th. V.40 I had lunch today at Josephine Baker&#39;s magnificent villa near Paris. I was supposed to do a story, but she was too sad; she didn&#39;t want to see me. My poor Bobchen, you&#39;ve had a fever and you&#39;re not receiving my letters. I&#39;m writing to you every day now; everything is fine between us. I&#39;ve already written to your friend at the Censorship Office. He&#39;s surely received it by now. Don&#39;t send me any money. I&#39;ve already told you I have enough for now, and the State has promised to pay me soon. I&#39;ve already sent you the Ilford bill. Cash the money, but don&#39;t keep it; I&#39;ll send it back to you. If so, I... You mustn&#39;t be without money, but be careful so it doesn&#39;t get stolen. Goodbye my darling Bobchen, I adore you and I can&#39;t stop thinking about you while holding you very, very tightly in my arms - Baba. I think the baby moved for the first time today.

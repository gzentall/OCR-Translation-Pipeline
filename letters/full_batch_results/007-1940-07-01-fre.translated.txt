Villeneuve Pot 1.7.40 My darling, We are still here and are waiting for our order for demobilization. I would be happy if I were or found. Naturally, I want to go straight to the hospital if I don&#39;t hear from you in the meantime. I think we are still here 2-3 days. It&#39;s terrible always waiting. But for the moment there is nothing to do, demobilization papers. I adore you my little wife and see you very soon. Without hope.

17.7.20 My dear, I haven&#39;t received any news yet, but I wanted to write to you that I was at the police station today and they told me that you can go to Aydle without any difficulty, just with a pass from Rignac. Flors qu sombe. very well (because of my Bracke, I&#39;m still waiting for news from Cans d&#39;essence). I asked for permission today to look for my wife. If I get it, I&#39;ll leave right away to look for you. I think it will be about 8 days. The expiry. Goodbye my dear, see you soon, Bob. To Mrs. E. Zweigenthal, dear.

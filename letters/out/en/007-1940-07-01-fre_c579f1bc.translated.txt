Villeneuve Pot 1.7.40 My dear, We are still here and we are waiting for our order for demobilization. I would be happy if I were here. Naturally I want to go to the fork of the mite to Higiniac if I don&#39;t have any news of faith. I cross, we are still 2-3 days here. It&#39;s terrible always waiting. But for the moment there is nothing to do, the demobilization papers. I adore you my little wife and see you soon. Without hope.

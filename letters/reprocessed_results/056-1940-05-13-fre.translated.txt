My love, In Paris, the 13th. V.40. These days are a little difficult and full of emotion, and your letters, which now arrive regularly (who knows for how long), are such a comfort, such a relief for me. Only now I wonder if you will stay where you are, and if you will be gone for long. It&#39;s best not to think about it; I try to think about it as little as possible, but I will only truly be at peace the day you are near me again... I don&#39;t know if I&#39;ve already told you that your photograph gave me immense pleasure. It&#39;s not a &quot;very good&quot; photo, but I like it enormously!! It&#39;s so you—I see you as I love you—I carry it in my bag and look at it from time to time. It always makes me feel a little better—I would like you to send me two or three 9x12 prints (good prints) of the photo of the two of us. Ask the Censor if you can send them so they&#39;re guaranteed to arrive. I&#39;m enclosing the original invoice from Illford. I don&#39;t want to send any more—but keep the money there; I have enough for now, and I hope the General Staff will pay us eventually. I haven&#39;t gone yet because you wrote that Spira won&#39;t be here before the 15th. If there&#39;s a chance, I&#39;ll go tomorrow, but without Spira, I&#39;ll only go if absolutely necessary. Spira could have taken care of things before leaving. Yesterday, Hélène didn&#39;t come because her brother-in-law, the doctor, arrived on leave (lucky him!), and I went with Mom and the Renards to Montparnasse for coffee. The Renards are having a lot of trouble. Dagny is selling me two suits, a coat, two pairs of shoes, and a bag—expensive, but they&#39;re having a lot of trouble finding exactly what they&#39;re looking for. I told them yesterday that during my next leave (when, for God&#39;s sake??) I&#39;ll need the apartment because I want to set up Thomas&#39;s room (!) in the office. They were surprised and said it threw all their plans into disarray because they were planning to move back to the apartment on Rue du Faubourg next winter. I said I was terribly sorry but that I needed a room now. Bubi wants me to wait until September with the move because Dagny is going on vacation in July. Bubi wants to live there alone and only get an apartment in the fall, but I absolutely want it done while I&#39;m here, because if I leave for a season or another, I&#39;ll be left alone with all this work and I&#39;ll never get it over with. Well, we&#39;ll see. Mom received a lovely letter this morning from Mrs. Freud, asking her to go to Agde for some sunshine. But neither Piste nor I see any point in her going. There are alerts everywhere right now, and she&#39;ll be terribly worried there. Here at least we&#39;re together. Not many people stay here. Anyway, Liebling, that&#39;s all for today. Write to me regularly like you have been these days. You know what it means to receive letters every day, and I&#39;m all yours. I love you.

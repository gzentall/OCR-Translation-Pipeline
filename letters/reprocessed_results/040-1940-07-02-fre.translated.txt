Liginiae, the 2nd, 01446 My darling, my Bobchen Lieber, my yout Finally I have received news that confirms my concern for your fate. I received the letter and his telegram from Perrigueux, but I didn&#39;t know you were in terrible conditions, heading towards a dreadful country. Your letter from Villeneuve-sur-Lot relieved me. If you weren&#39;t there, I wouldn&#39;t have known you would have spent a few days away from me—where you are. You are in France, where you are obviously being directed towards the Pyrenees. I don&#39;t mind finding you again, and then, provided that I can soon live a modest but bearable life with you, maintaining a happy game. I am asked if I wrote to your JP and also to Perrigueux, where unfortunately I couldn&#39;t telegraph; it was difficult. You must not have received my letters; I have a thousand and one things to say. I know where you went today; I simply want to write this little note to the towns you indicated. We are all still in Léginice, so rest assured, Alois, we haven&#39;t sworn off here, we&#39;ve never moved. We intend to stay here for at least another two months. We are gradually settling in, and everything is going relatively well, so there&#39;s no reason to change places. Betty and Etienne will go to Paris later to see how things are going with our apartments. We will probably stay here – I myself am making various requests. Can you tell me what you want me to look for (to drive the car or wherever I go to where you are?). I think the Hérault is much better for us than the Corrèze (especially in winter if we are allowed to stay there or perhaps in Béziers). I&#39;m waiting to be able to go to Masauté. Is Masauté well? I&#39;ve just had a few horrible days, but not hearing from you is the most dreadful thing I know. It&#39;s worse than... I read my kind letter from Mrs. Blumenstein and U. Zentall. My Bobchen, I think of you all day. My only desire is to see you again very soon. I love you like crazy. &quot;Son&quot; Like a S to love your Babe. Зава LIGI POSTES REPUBLIQUE FRANÇAISE M. Robert Zentall Poste Restante Agde (Hérault) esau Thal Plus. EE Zentall Liginial (Corrèze) AG) 12 H -5-7 ET 1940 E
